Rohan Mehta

rohanmehta@g[dot]harvard[dot]edu

I am a PhD student at Harvard University, where I am advised by Mikhail Lukin. I work in quantum error correction and am broadly interested in developing useful quantum computers. Previously, I completed my undergraduate degree at the University of Chicago, where I was advised by Liang Jiang.
